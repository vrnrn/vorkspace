# Vorkspaces, in plain English

A vorkspace is a bundle of the folders and Bots you use for one kind of work, like "Main" or "Side projects". The name at the top left of the window shows which one you are in. Click it to switch, or choose **Manage vorkspaces** to make, change or delete one.

The same folder can be in more than one vorkspace, and its sessions show up in each. Taking a folder out of a vorkspace only hides it there; nothing is deleted. Bots belong to one vorkspace each: a Bot you create joins the vorkspace you are in, and adding it to another vorkspace moves it there. The first time you open this version, all your existing folders and Bots go into one vorkspace called Main.

The left side now lists sessions instead of projects. You see five at a time: anything running or waiting for approval first, then pinned and the latest. The arrow under the list shows all of them, and the list scrolls when it runs out of room. Each card shows which AI ran it, which project it is in, and the Bot that started it, if any. Above the list, the magnifier searches titles, the filter narrows by status, time, AI, project or a Bot by name, and the plus starts a new session. Right-click a card to pin, rename, archive or delete it, hover to peek at it, or drag it into the workspace to open it side by side. Session folders are gone. To pick a different project, use the project name at the top of the sidebar. Use the button beside the project name for project actions like archiving. A Bot’s panel opens its launched sessions and documents, including sessions it keeps out of the rail.

Opening a conversation from another vorkspace through Search or the Back shortcut switches to a vorkspace that contains it. A new session starts in the checkout shown by the chat you are working in. Connector automations keep watching their projects while you work in another vorkspace.

**Manage vorkspaces…** also has a tab for each project in the vorkspace, with its code-index and notification settings. It lets you rename a vorkspace, pick its color and a chat background picture shown behind all its sessions, add or remove its folders and Bots, switch to it, or delete it. You always keep at least one.

Projects themselves are no longer customized: each is a folder icon with its own automatic color and its folder name. Colors and backgrounds belong to the vorkspace instead.

The sidebar with Explorer, Changes and To-dos has its own folder button in the top bar, next to the rail button (or press ⇧⌘B). It stays open or closed the way you left it, whatever you are looking at.

Each vorkspace is meant to get its own window. That is being built in steps. Already, the app keeps one shared list of vorkspaces for all windows, lets only one window run reminders and automations, and never lets two windows run the same session. Bots, voice and a few other background parts still need the same treatment before a second window can open.

The [technical guide](../vorkspaces.md) has the details.

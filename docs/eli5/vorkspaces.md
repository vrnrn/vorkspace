# Vorkspaces, in plain English

A vorkspace is a bundle of the folders and Bots you use for one kind of work, like "Main" or "Side projects". The name at the top left of the window shows which one you are in. Click it to switch, or choose **Manage vorkspaces** to make, change or delete one.

The same folder can be in more than one vorkspace, and its sessions show up in each. Taking a folder out of a vorkspace only hides it there; nothing is deleted. Bots belong to one vorkspace each: a Bot you create joins the vorkspace you are in, and adding it to another vorkspace moves it there. The first time you open this version, all your existing folders and Bots go into one vorkspace called Main.

The left rail shows five sessions at a time. Anything running or waiting for approval comes first, followed by pinned and recent conversations. The arrow under the list shows more. Each card shows its AI and project, along with its folder and the Bot that started it when those apply. Search finds sessions by title or folder name. The filter can narrow the list, and the plus starts a new session.

Right-click a card to pin, rename, move, archive, or delete it. **Move to folder** lets you pick a folder, create one, or remove the session from its current folder. Select several conversations from the same project to move them together. The change appears in your other open windows. Hover to peek at a conversation, or drag it into the workspace to open it side by side.

Use the project name at the top of the sidebar to switch projects. The button beside it opens project actions such as archiving. A Bot's panel opens its launched sessions and documents, including sessions it keeps out of the rail.

Opening a conversation from another vorkspace through Search or the Back shortcut switches to a vorkspace that contains it. A new session starts in the checkout shown by the chat you are working in. Connector automations keep watching their projects while you work in another vorkspace.

**Manage vorkspaces…** also has a tab for each project in the vorkspace, with its code-index and notification settings. It lets you rename a vorkspace, pick its color and a chat background picture shown behind all its sessions, add or remove its folders and Bots, switch to it, or delete it. You always keep at least one. A picture you choose here takes precedence over the global chat picture. Bots have their own chat pictures.

Under **Look**, type up to two **Icon letters** to tell your vorkspaces apart at a glance. Leave the field blank to use the first letter of the vorkspace name. Each project shows its folder name and a folder icon with an automatic color.

The sidebar with Explorer, Changes and To-dos has its own folder button in the top bar, next to the rail button (or press ⇧⌘B). It stays open or closed the way you left it, whatever you are looking at.

Use **File → New Window** (⇧⌘N) to open another window in your current vorkspace. To start in a different vorkspace, use **Open in New Window** in the switcher. Each window keeps its own tabs, layout and zoom. Projects, Bots, reminders and most settings are shared. If you open a conversation that is already live, the app brings its window forward.

Quit the app to reopen the same window layouts next time. **Window → Close Window** (⇧⌘W) closes just the current window, while ⌘W closes a tab. Finish or stop any active work before closing the window that owns it.

If a note changed in another window while you were editing it, you can save your edits as a copy or reload the latest version.

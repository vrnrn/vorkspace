# A consistent look across pages

Notes, Automations, GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Todos and Search share the same compact controls, text sizes, spacing and colors. Meeting recordings live inside Notes. Page titles are easy to distinguish from content, and secondary details remain readable in light and dark mode. Buttons and tabs use the same sizing and keyboard focus treatment. Corners follow the same pattern across pages: tighter on small controls, softer on cards and panes, and rounder on floating dialogs. Hover highlights keep the shape of the control they belong to.

Filters sit quietly in their toolbar until you click or focus them to type. The field background disappears when you move focus elsewhere. Press **+** above the Notes list to create a note. Every note offers **Start recording** and a choice of audio sources. Opening a blank note or capturing no words in an unnamed recording does not add an empty saved note.

Pages can open as tabs beside your conversations. Different notes and connector items can each have a tab, with their own unfinished text and reading position. Drag tabs into a split to keep related work together. Reopening a page already beside a project’s conversation brings that project and layout back together. An agent in another project cannot use that page to open the unrelated conversation. Search keeps your query when you switch projects, but searches and opens files from the new working copy. Clicking a session or document in a Bot's list opens it beside the Bot when there is room, or in its own tab when there is not.

In wide panes, the document pane curves at the top and bottom where it meets the list, matching the rest of the workspace. When a Notes, Inbox or Automations pane becomes narrow, it lets you move between the list and the selected item even if the window is wide. The same applies when those pages fill a narrow window. Going Back keeps your unfinished text and reading position. Recording updates do not close the list or jump your note back to the top. You can switch to your written text while a recording loads, and its transcript remains available when loading finishes. A tab you choose while loading stays selected. If you choose another note while saving, deleting, or discarding edits, the app remembers both the note and tab. Refreshes keep that choice. If deletion or discarding fails, the app can save the retained edits before opening the note you chose. Automation templates stack, to-do columns wrap to fit their tile, and long search paths fit within the available space.

In a note, **Preview** shows your written text and **Source** edits it. An attached recording adds **Summary** and **Transcript** beside them. The active tab has a line underneath its name. **Edit summary** lets you change the generated summary without changing your authored note text.

Summaries and authored note text use the same native formatting, and you can select and copy their text. Summaries save the finished response after the provider succeeds. They cannot load hidden images or open files on your Mac. Your authored notes and connector items keep their normal attachment features. In **Settings → Connectors → Notes**, you can choose the summary provider, model, effort and prompt, or turn Notes and recording controls off while keeping saved data.

A note can start with a dropped image. A failed image import leaves a blank draft unsaved. Deleting a recorded note saves the deletion before cleaning up its recording files, so closing the window cannot leave a broken note behind. If cleanup is interrupted, opening Notes retries it without bringing the note back.

Settings lists **App**, **Agents**, then **Connectors**. Turning a plugin off hides its settings button and page, including from search. Turn it back on in Plugins to restore them. Pages use the same cards and spacing as Providers. A page introduces its purpose once; the controls explain only what changes or what you need to know. Model menus show the selected model directly. Recording summaries also offer the model’s supported effort levels, with **Default** leaving that choice to the provider.

The [technical guide](../native-pages.md) records the shared sizes and implementation boundaries. Click a workspace icon to open its sidebar; moving the mouse over the icons or their edge does not open a floating preview.

A Bot keeps its sessions and documents in a floating island on the right of its own pane. The island collapses before it would squeeze the chat’s normal width. Click the Bot face or the small top-right button to open it. The header shows what the Bot is doing and has a settings gear. Sessions show up to five active sessions, with the most recently updated first, or your three most recent sessions when nothing is running. Documents show the three most recently updated. Click a section’s down chevron to give it the full island and scroll through the rest; the up chevron brings both lists back. The island is slightly more opaque than the pane behind it. To change a Bot’s appearance, open its settings and click the pencil on its avatar, or choose its look when creating it. Pick its shape, face, colours and a printed pattern for its body (dither, halftone, grain, stripes or plain); each pattern choice shows the Bot wearing it. Habits are in Bot settings too. The app’s main sidebar always stays on the left, with a continuous background across its compact rail and top navigation. When you hover over or drag the edge of a note or connector list, the highlight follows the pane’s rounded corners.

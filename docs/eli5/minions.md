# Minions, explained simply

Minions are an experiment you can turn on in **Settings › Bots**. When they're on, a Bot can hand pieces of a job to little helpers called minions instead of doing everything itself. Just mention "minions" when you ask a Bot for something and it will split the work up.

Each minion works in its own session inside one of the Bot's projects, so you'll see it in the project's list of sessions with a small tag showing which Bot it belongs to. Minions share their Bot's memory: whatever one of them learns and saves, the Bot and the other minions can read too. They also share the Bot's to-do board.

When the minions are done, the Bot gets a report and pulls everything together. Minions can't start more sessions, make more minions, or change their Bot's personality or habits.
